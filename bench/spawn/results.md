# Thread spawn benchmarks: results

Generated by `bench/spawn/spawn_bench.py`; the design, the cost model and the predictions are in [design.md](design.md). Times are per unit (a spawn + wait unless the row says otherwise), the median over batches measured in-process; `~` marks a point whose p90/p10 spread stayed above 1.5x after one re-run.

Machine: Apple M5, 10 hardware threads, Darwin 25.6.0. C toolchain: clang 21.0.0. Measured 2026-10-09 14:10.
